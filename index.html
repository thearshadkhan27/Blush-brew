The Blush & Brew Story

A Sanctuary for Coffee & Soul in Amravati

Situated on Pote College Road in Vijay Colony, near Orchid Bakery, Blush & Brew was imagined as an antidote to noisy, rushed chain cafes. We crafted an intimate space where deep charcoal aesthetics, warm amber illumination, and the fragrant steam of specialty Arabica unite into a soothing haven.

Our name reflects our commitment: 'Blush' for heartfelt moments, floral notes, and blushing sweetness; 'Brew' for precise extraction and unhurried craftsmanship.
